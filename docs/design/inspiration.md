## Selected direction

Use Google Sans and the supplied Google colors.
Use white surfaces and flat geometric artwork.
Use Google I/O for heading hierarchy and event character.
Use Guelph and Kalyani for community content and small graphic details.
See [the implemented design](../design.md) for the section rules.

## Proposed home-page structure

1. Show the club name and a short purpose statement.
2. Give visitors a clear action to join the community.
3. Show confirmed upcoming events and the past event archive.
4. Introduce all eight departments and their leads in a static grid.
5. Show a community photo gallery.
6. Give visitors community links and common answers.
7. Keep alumni and member sign-in links in the footer.

## Design details to compare

- Compare navigation on desktop and mobile screens.
- Check how each site uses empty space between sections.
- Compare heading sizes and body-text widths.
- Look at image shapes and card layouts.
- Check how each site marks its primary action.
- Compare color use across ordinary and highlighted sections.
- Check keyboard focus and hover states during implementation.
